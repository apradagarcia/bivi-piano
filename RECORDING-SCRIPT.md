# Recording script: Bivi's piano, Bivi's iPhone, Bivi's room

About 10 minutes. These recordings tell us, with real numbers, whether the iPhone mic is good enough or whether we need the cable.

## Before you start

**iPhone 16**
1. Settings > Apps > Voice Memos > **Audio Quality: Lossless**
2. Put the iPhone **on the music rest, where she'll keep it while practicing**, screen facing her.
3. Don't cover the bottom edge (that's where the mic is).

**Casiotone CT-S1**
1. Turn it on. It starts on **Grand Piano**, which is the sound we want.
2. Volume where she normally plays. No headphones, metronome off.

**The room:** Normal, the way it is when she practices. Windows and fans as usual.

## Rules
- One recording per section, in Voice Memos. **Rename each one** exactly as shown (e.g. `2 keys medium`).
- Leave **about 2 seconds** between notes and hold each one about 1 second (except sections 6 and 7).
- **Wrong note? Stop, delete that recording, and redo the section.** The order is how we know which note is which.
- The keyboard has 61 keys and **6 C's** (Do). Counting C's from the left: the **1st C** is the lowest key, the **3rd C is middle C**.

## The 7 recordings

### 1 silence (90 seconds, don't play)
- 0:00 to 0:30: room quiet
- 0:30 to 1:00: turn on a fan or the AC, and tap the table 5 or 6 times
- 1:00 to 1:30: TV on, or someone talking normally

### 2 keys medium (about 2 minutes)
**Every key, white and black, one at a time, from the lowest key to the highest** (61 notes), medium strength.

### 3 keys soft (about 1 minute 15 seconds)
**Every key, white and black, from the 2nd C to the 5th C** (37 notes), **softly**, the way she plays a gentle part.

### 4 keys loud (about 45 seconds)
**White keys only, from the 2nd C to the 5th C** (22 notes), **strongly**.

### 5 chords (about 2 minutes)
Each chord **3 times**, about 2 seconds apart. All notes together. "Middle" = around middle C.

| # | Chord | Keys (left to right) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | C | C E G (Do Mi Sol), starting on middle C |
| 2 | F/C | C F A (Do Fa La), starting on middle C |
| 3 | G/B | B D G (Si Re Sol), B just below middle C |
| 4 | Am | C E A (Do Mi La), starting on middle C |
| 5 | Dm | D F A (Re Fa La), D just above middle C |
| 6 | Em | E G B (Mi Sol Si), E above middle C |
| 7 | G7 | G B F (Sol Si Fa), G below middle C |
| 8 | C both hands | Left hand: the C below middle C. Right hand: C E G from middle C |
| 9 | F both hands | Left hand: the lowest F on the keyboard. Right hand: C F A from middle C |
| 10 | G both hands | Left hand: the lowest G on the keyboard. Right hand: B D G (B below middle C) |
| 11 | Am both hands | Left hand: the lowest A on the keyboard. Right hand: C E A from middle C |
| 12 | C-E | Middle C + the E above it |
| 13 | C-G | Middle C + the G above it |
| 14 | Octave low | The C below middle C + middle C |
| 15 | Octave high | Middle C + the C above it |

### 6 repeats (about 10 seconds)
- **Middle C 8 times**, steady, about 2 per second
- Short pause, then **the E above middle C 8 times**, a bit faster

### 7 scale (about 10 seconds)
**White keys from middle C up to the 5th C, then back down to middle C** (29 notes), steady, about 3 notes per second, like a calm scale.

## Sending them
AirDrop the 7 recordings to the Mac, then upload them here. The importer finds every note on its own, checks the labels by pitch, and tells us if anything needs redoing.

*Keeping the surprise:* you can record this yourself on her CT-S1 in her room (same phone model is best, but her phone is ideal), or tell her you're "testing a piano app." The recordings are only used for testing and aren't included in the app.
